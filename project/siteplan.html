About This Site

Dekiru (できる)! Meaning "You can do it!", a Japanese language learning site for those who want to start forming sentences quickly!

  • Practice common phrases.
  • Learn language structure.
  • Understand how particles work.
  • Identify particles in a sentance.

Common Phrases

Some examples of phrases that you will come across in the lessons!

こんにちは (konnichiwa)

”は (wa)” Marks the subject of the sentence. In the word ”こんにちは” it does not mark a subject but is apart of the word.

元気ですか (ogenki desu ka)

”か (ka)” turning the sentence into a question.

ありがとうございます (arigatōgozaimasu)

”ございます (gozaimasu)” a more formal or polite equivilant of ある.
